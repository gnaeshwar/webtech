Before You Register

Instructions & Eligibility

Registration Steps

  1. Review the course catalog above.
  2. Confirm you meet the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the registration form with accurate details.
  4. Select one or more courses for the term.
  5. Submit and review your registration summary.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Must be an actively enrolled student.
  • Semester value must be between 1 and 8.
  • No outstanding fee dues on record.
  • Elective courses require department approval.
